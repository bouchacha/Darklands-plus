New encounters
Eighty new encounters in the spirit of the original: 50 in the wild (gallows mandrakes, flagellants, robber knights, kobolds in the mines, a hermit, a Schrat's oak, the Wild Hunt and more), 10 on the river boats, 12 in the towns' streets (a cutpurse, the night watch, a fire) and 8 in camp at night. In the wild they come up among the game's own random encounters, about as often as one more entry of its table each; on a boat trip three times in ten, in a street now and then, after a night in camp one time in eight. A small seal in the corner of the card marks a new one, and under the text a choice ends on, what came of it: each member's changes, things, money, fame, a town's opinion and the time it took.
