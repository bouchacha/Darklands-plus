New encounters
A hundred and sixty new encounters in the spirit of the original: 90 in the wild (gallows mandrakes, flagellants, robber knights, kobolds in the mines, a hermit, a Schrat's oak, the Devil's bridge, a moss-wife, the sleeping emperor, the Wild Hunt and more), 18 on the river boats, 34 in the towns' streets (a cutpurse, the night watch, a fire, the popinjay shoot, the Fools' Court) and 18 in camp. In the wild they come up among the game's own random encounters, about as often as one more entry of its table each; on a boat trip three times in ten, in a street now and then, after a night in camp one time in eight. A small seal in the corner of the card marks a new one, and under the text a choice ends on, what came of it: each member's changes, things, money, fame, a town's opinion and the time it took.
