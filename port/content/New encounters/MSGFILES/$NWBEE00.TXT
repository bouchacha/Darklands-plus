The moss flares up instead of smouldering, and the bees come out not drowsy but furious. You flee, flailing, with a humming cloud at your heels, and it is a long while before anyone can see out of both eyes.

=== card 5: 10 10 0 240 0 ===
As the light fails, the humming sinks to a drowsy murmur. Moving softly, you take the comb without a single sting, and eat it by your fire that night.
=== card 6: 10 10 0 240 0 ===
As dusk falls you creep up to the tree. Someone treads on a dry stick; the murmur rises to a roar, and the bees pour out into the twilight after you.
=== card 7: 10 10 0 240 0 ===
