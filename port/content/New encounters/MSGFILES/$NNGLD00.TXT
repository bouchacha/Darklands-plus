But down the side, by the back shutter, a lantern hooded with a cloth throws a slit of light, and something grates on iron: a bar, working at a hinge. Three shapes crouch there, and a fourth keeps watch at the corner.

The masters of the guild, roused from their beds, find their hall unbroken and the strangers who saved it, and are suitably grateful.
