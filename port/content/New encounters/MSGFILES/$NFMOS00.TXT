A thin voice calls from a fresh stump beside the path: "Good folk! Good folk!" On the stump sits a little old woman no bigger than a child of four, wrapped in moss and lichen, with a face like a winter apple and hair like grey beard-moss.
\14$ChosenOneName has heard of such: the moss-wives, the little women of the forest. The Wild Huntsman hunts them, and they are safe only on a stump with three crosses cut into it, as woodcutters once knew to do.
\14"They have forgotten the crosses," she says, "and I am so tired. Cut me three crosses, good folk, or give me a crust of bread. Only no caraway in it! None of that cursed caraway!"
