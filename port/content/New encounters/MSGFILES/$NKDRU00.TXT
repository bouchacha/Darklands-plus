At first light the horses stand trembling at their tethers, flanks white with dried lather, as if they had been ridden hard all night. Their eyes roll at every sound. All down their necks the manes are twisted into tight little knotted loops, each just big enough for a small foot.

Now that you think of it, none of you slept well, and every one of you woke with a weight on the chest.
