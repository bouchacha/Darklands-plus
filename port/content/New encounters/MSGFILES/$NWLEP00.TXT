\14And whose prayers would God hear more gladly than theirs?

The lepers shrink back in astonishment as $ChosenOneName comes among them. $He washes their sores with wine, dresses them with salve and clean linen, and binds their feet so they can walk.

The lepers pray with you, but no sign is given. They thank you all the same.

You shout and throw stones until the lepers stumble away into the bushes, their clapper rattling. The law is on your side. Whether God is, is another matter.
