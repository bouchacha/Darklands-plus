\14You sleep peacefully until dawn.
=== card 4: 10 10 0 240 0 ===
You pray, but the lights dance on, mocking and restless.

You make camp, set a watch and keep your eyes from the marsh. "If they want company," grumbles $ChosenOneName, "they can come to the fire like honest folk." By dawn the lights are gone.
