# $NVHRS00: the frightened horse (New encounters)

A horse-dealer is taking three horses down the river, tethered amidships among the bales: two cobs and a tall grey stallion he boasts is worth forty florins. Toward noon a gadfly settles on the grey's neck. He screams, rears, snaps his halter like a thread and lashes out with both hind hooves. A boatman goes down. The boat heels until the water slops over the gunwale.
\14The dealer has climbed onto the cabin roof and is shouting advice. "Hold him, somebody! Don't hurt him!"
