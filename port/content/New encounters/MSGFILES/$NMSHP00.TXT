# $NMSHP00: the ship-mill adrift (New encounters)
=== card 0: 10 10 0 240 0 ===
Shouts and lanterns on the river bank in the dark. The river is running high and fast after a week of rain, and out on the black water a ship-mill has torn loose from its moorings: a mill-house built across two moored hulls, its great wheel still turning in the current as it drifts. A light burns in its window, and downstream, not half a mile off, stands the stone bridge.
\14The miller runs along the bank in his nightshirt with a coil of rope, howling to every saint he knows. "My mill! It will break on the piers!"

$ChosenOneName swings the rope twice round $his head and lets fly. The loop drops over a post of the mill's gallery, every hand on the bank seizes the rope, and slowly, groaning, the mill swings in and grinds onto the mud.
