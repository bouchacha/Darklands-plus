In a hamlet of three smoke-blackened houses high on the mountain, a woman runs out into the snow to stop you. Her husband's mother died at Martinmas. The ground is frozen as hard as iron and the path down to the parish church is buried, so the old woman lies in the woodshed under a sheet, frozen stiff, waiting for the thaw, as the dead always wait up here.
