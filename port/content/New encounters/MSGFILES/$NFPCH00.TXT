# $NFPCH00: the pitch-burners (New encounters)

Among the dark pines a red glow, and a smell of resin so thick you can taste it: pitch-burners. Pine resin bubbles in clay pots over a fire pit, and three men and a boy, sticky to the elbows, are ladling the black pitch into barrels for the boatmen and the coopers.
\14Their headman looks at your weapons with something like hope. "Every new moon a pack of rogues comes down from the hills for a barrel and our coin. Tonight is the night. Stay with us, masters, for God's sake. Ladles are no match for spears."
