# $NHWBL00: the weather bell (New encounters)
=== card 0: 10 10 0 240 0 ===
Above a village of shingled roofs, a little chapel crowns a crag, reached by steps cut in the rock. Black clouds are piling over the peaks, and the light has turned a sickly yellow: hail weather, the kind that flattens a year's barley in a quarter of an hour.
\14Villagers are shouting up from the fields. The weather bell must be rung, the bell the bishop blessed against storms and the devils that ride in them. But the sexton is old and lame, and everyone knows the lightning seeks out bell-ringers.
\14You...
\14\15...\1drun up the steps and ring the bell yourselves.
\15...\1drig the rope so it can be rung from the shelter of the stair.
\16...\1dpray to a saint to turn the storm aside.
\15...\1dtake shelter from the storm in the chapel.
\15...\1dhurry on before the storm breaks.

$ChosenOneName hauls on the bell rope until $his palms blister, while the thunder cracks overhead and the bell shouts back at it. Then the cloud splits over the crag. The hail falls white on the bare rock to either side, and the barley below gets nothing worse than rain.
\14The villagers climb up with a jug of beer and their thanks. They will speak of you in the town.

\14Afterwards the villagers come up and look at the rope, and at you. Then they go home. Their tale will be in the town before you are.
