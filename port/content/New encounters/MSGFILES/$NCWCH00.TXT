# $NCWCH00: the forest child (New encounters)
=== card 0: 10 10 0 240 0 ===
At first light you find your food sack slit open and half the bread gone. A small, thin figure crouches at the edge of the clearing, gnawing at a heel of it: a child of nine or ten, barefoot, in rags and a cloak of rabbit pelts, its hair matted with leaves. When it sees you looking, it bares its teeth like a fox. But it does not run.

\14\15...\1dcoax it closer with food and kind words.
\15...\1dlet it run, and follow it into the forest.
\15...\1dchase it off with shouts and a thrown stick.

$ChosenOneName sits down a little way off and holds out bread and cheese, talking softly, as to a strayed foal. Slowly the child creeps closer, snatches, eats, and creeps closer again. It is a girl. She has a name, Els, and a dead mother somewhere in the forest, and very little else.

You follow the child into the forest, but it slips through the undergrowth like a fox, and within the hour you have lost it and the path as well. It is midday before you find your way back to your camp, scratched and weary.
