\14"In the next valley, over the border, a free company has been camped three days," he says, "waiting to learn how many spears hold this keep. Five is the answer: two old men, two boys and me. My lord sends neither men nor pay. Stand on my walls tonight, and let them count you."

All night you tramp the walls with torches, swapping cloaks and hats and calling to one another in different voices, while the boys beat a kettle like a drum. At dawn the free company's fires are cold, and their tracks lead back the way they came.
\14The castellan has no pay to give you, but he swears the town will hear your names.
