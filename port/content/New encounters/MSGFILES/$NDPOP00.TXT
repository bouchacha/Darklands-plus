A drummer in the colours of the crossbowmen's guild is beating his way down the street, and half the town is following him to the shooting ground under the walls. Today is the popinjay shoot. A painted wooden bird sits on a pole as tall as a church tower, and whoever brings it down is king of the shooters for a year.
