\14\15...\1dbreak the seal and read it.

$ChosenOneName breaks the seal and reads slowly, lips moving. "Fetch it, and tell no one."

\14\15...\1ddig up the silver for yourselves.

\14Young Weidner will wait a long time for his inheritance.
