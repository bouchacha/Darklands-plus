Darklands+ runs Darklands (MicroProse, 1992) natively on Windows, without DOSBox, from your own copy of the game. It adds the things players have asked for over the years, and every addition can be switched off. **F12 > Helpers > "Play the original game"** turns all of them off at once.

**You need** your own Darklands 483.07 (GOG or Steam) and 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. This download contains nothing from the game.

**Install:** unpack the zip anywhere and start `darklands.exe`. It finds a GOG or Steam installation by itself or asks for the folder once. The first start takes about two minutes while Darklands+ translates the game program from your `DARKLAND.EXE`; after that it starts at once. Your saved games stay where they always were. `Darklands+ README.txt` in the zip has the details.

**New in 1.2:** more of the game's screens are drawn by Darklands+ itself (each can be switched off in F12 > Helpers; "Play the original game" turns them all off). The **shops** show everything for sale and everything the member carries in two lists, with Buy and Sell on each row (Shift: ten / the whole stack; a click on a member's coin makes them the one who pays), prices you cannot pay in red, what is in hand or worn marked "in use", and a strip at the bottom describing whatever the pointer is on instead of tooltips over the lists. The **character sheet** is drawn anew: the game's paper doll, put together from its own pictures as you equip things, the load under it, and beside it the things (those in use first; the quality only where it can differ) with Ready, Unequip, Drink and Drop (with Undo) on each row; a thing dragged onto a member in the party panel is given to them (Shift: one of a stack). The attributes and skills fit on one page in the original's groups, with their signs. The **saints** of the whole party are on one page: who knows each, a choice of what they help with, and the saint's statue from the game's prayer screen beside its legend and effect. The **formulae** of the whole party likewise: who knows each, the ingredients against what the party carries, and each alchemist's chance. It works in battle too. The game's **menus** (Game, Orders, Attack, Party) open with a right click or F10 and stay open until you choose an entry or click elsewhere, instead of only while the right button is held; their entries are still the game's own. **Save** asks for the comment on a page that lists the saves there are, and **Load** lists them all to choose from, each with where and when it was made in the game, who is in the party and how long ago it was written (the saving and loading are still the game's own). The **party panel** at the left is drawn anew wherever it shows: labelled Endurance, Strength and Divine Favor bars with their numbers, the order of each member in a battle, and a picture: your own portrait (portraits\NAME.png in the Darklands+ folder), or the member's battle figure in their colours (from the start, not only after a battle), or the herald (F12 > Helpers). The **loot** after a battle shows the whole pile, takes a whole stack in one click, and has Take all and Share out; the thing under the pointer is described below the lists, with about what a shop pays for it. **Making a character** is one page per step (name and gender, childhood, attributes, profession, skills) with the character as it stands beside it and what the childhood or profession under the pointer would bring: the attributes after it (and what the years past thirty cost), each skill's gain and the room left, the virtues and saints learned; + and - on each attribute or skill, the name typed in place. **Choosing the party** of a new game is one page too: the four places with each member's figure in their colours and their Endurance, Strength and Divine Favor, the characters kept (click to choose, double-click to add or drop), and the chosen one's attributes, best skills and what they know and carry, with Add or Drop, the sheet, the figure, herald and colours, and Delete (asked twice); New character and Begin along the bottom; each member added gets a look of their own (a woman the woman figure, everyone the next colours) instead of all the same tall man. The **inn's party screen** (find somebody to join) is the same page: those kept at the inn, Add or Stays here, a new character, the looks and the sheet, Done. At an inn's party screen too: a member who retired and joins again comes back with their own figure and colours (the game gave everyone who joined the tall man in the same colours), and a newcomer gets a look of their own. **Party Info** shows the party's fame and its title, the time, where the party is, its wealth, and its reputation in every city at once (by reputation or by name), instead of one town at a time under the pointer. **Change Marching Order** is a list to move members up and down, instead of clicking each in turn. **A room at the inn** (and a camp) is one page: each member with strength and divine favor and the day chosen for them, the activities they can choose (greyed, with the reason, where not), the town's teachers with their fees a day, what a day costs against the purse, and A day, **A week**, **Until rested** (days until nobody is regaining strength or praying, at most 14) and Leave (several days pass without the hourglass for each); the choices are still the game's own. **Enemy Info** (E in a battle) shows every kind of foe on the field with its picture, how many there are and still fight, its armour, shield and weapons, and whether each of your weapons pierces its armour, is halved or is deflected (the original's two kinds were drawn under the new party panel, hidden, while the battle waited for a key). The travel map's messages (entering a robber knight's land, a blighted country ...) are shown in the cards' style, and the click that closes one no longer walks the party. **Calling on a saint** shows the saint's statue, legend and what the saint does, the divine favor to spend with - and +, and the chance the saint hears, with Pray (for a chosen member where the saint helps one) and Don't pray. The **store room** of a town shows everything stored and everything the member carries in two lists, with Take and Put on each row (one or the whole stack) and Put away for everything not in use. **Alchemy** lists every formula the alchemist knows, with its ingredients needed and at hand (and, for one that is short, where the party has seen it sold: here, or the nearest town and its price; failing that, the nearest town whose shops will have it, marked "expected": a town's wares are fixed for the whole game; both can be switched off), the batches with - and +, and the chance of success with its parts. The stock, prices, equipping and chances are still the game's own. **Arrows, quarrels and balls never run out** (on by default, F12 > Helpers): the party's shots take nothing from the pack (one in the quiver is enough) and leave nothing to pick out of the loot afterwards; enemies shoot and lose theirs as before. Javelins, throwing axes, throwing knives and darts never run out either (a switch of their own). Also: thrown javelins, axes, knives and darts go back to their throwers after a battle (the 1.1.1 version did not recognise them; what was given back, or why not, is written in the log); the loot's rows say Take all and Take 1; travel to a town no longer stops beside it (a site's edge that enters nothing); a member's sheet opened in a battle no longer ends the battle log; item and saint tooltips stay inside the window; F10 opens the menus on the cards and the new screens too, and Alt+S, Alt+L and the game's other Alt keys work there; in a battle the member being given orders has the orders the game allows as buttons in the right margin (Std Attack, Vulnerable, Berserk, Parry, Missile, Walk, Halt ...); a click or the pointer on the margins no longer reaches the game (a battle map scrolled, a click walked the party to the picture's edge); in a battle the right margin no longer repeats the party's bars when the new party panel shows them. Fixed: with the New encounters mod on, travel could stop the game with "pics\xhouse3.pic: No such file" (the developers' test card, card 0) or meet the town guards' challenge in open country: the mod lost the map's own choice of what comes next when no new encounter was due. A guard also sends any request for card 0 on to the map or the town. Fixed (a bug of 483.07): the inn's Train or Study could list no teachers and show a cost like "-4096pfs" (and a purse that grew), then pass the day with nothing learnt, when another town's teachers were still about; it now lists the town's own teachers and charges their fee. Fixed (a bug of 483.07): when acid or fire wore down a member's armour in battle, every identical spare that member carried (identical things are kept as one stack) lost quality with it and was destroyed with it; now only the armour being worn suffers. The skills a battle, a lesson or a card raised are no longer hidden behind the party panel (where the game waited for a key unseen): each shows on the member's box for a few seconds and goes into the log. The shops, the loot, the store room and the sheet say what an ingredient goes into (and which of those formulae the party knows), what rope, lockpicks and instruments are for, and that the game has no use for torches, candles, lanterns, spikes, grapnels and clocks. At an alchemist who sells formulae, the option lists the formulae themselves with their prices and who in the party knows each already; its list names each formula (not just the potion), marks the members who know it, and its tooltip gives that formula's difficulty, its place among the potion's three formulae, who knows it and its ingredients. The formula tooltips everywhere say who in the party knows the formula. In a shop that sells ingredients, **Formulae** lists the formulae the party knows that use them: how many batches the party can make with what it carries, what a batch takes and what it costs here, and +1 / +5 buy what one or five batches more need (and say what must be found elsewhere). In a shop and in the loot, a weapon or an armour is set against what the chosen member has in that place now: the armour worn there (harder or softer, better or less well made), the shield carried, or the weapon in hand with the member's skill for this one and whether it is too heavy. Should Darklands ever stop on an error, Darklands+ now says so (instead of "the party's journey has ended") and no longer enters the party in the Honor Roll. After a fight in a street or a dungeon, the margin says how to go on (walk out of the place). A card's tenth option is chosen with 0. **F12 > Guide** explains how the game works for a new player (the game came with a thick manual): endurance, strength and divine favor, money and reputation, towns and inns, healing, alchemy, saints, battle, travel and saving, with the game's own numbers and no story. The **tasks** in the margin are a short title each ("Raubritter @ Altenberg", "Debt Note from Lübeck") with the days left; a click opens one (the whole task, its deadline, and which way and about how many days away it is) and closes it again, and a click on "by deadline" orders them by the nearest instead, or as taken. The journal's quest log has the same titles, opens the same way and keeps the same order. The world map (M) lists them too: a click shows the place, ready for Travel there. Fixed (bugs of 483.07, the "Card options greyed by mistake" switch): getting out over a city wall the guard could be offered a bribe the party could not pay; at a rebel crowd's fortress the climb with a rope was greyed when the party had a rope; one wall climb counted only the last member's rope; and at the wall by an old sally port each line from the second on did the next line's deed. On a card, an option shown greyed has a ? where its number would be, and the pointer on it says why it cannot be chosen now and what makes it possible (the guild will not see you again today, the gate must be broken first, you cannot afford the toll, the last member cannot retire ...): about 280 reasons read from the game's card scripts, a few of them oddities of the original kept as they are and named as such. On a card, a potion or saint in an option's list that cannot be chosen says why (the member knows the formula but carries none; at the easiest level the leader needs none), an option that starts a fight is marked with two crossed swords, and a text in a narrow column beside a picture starts with a plain letter instead of the large initial (which left a word or two a line). During the loot after a battle the margin still shows where the party is and its tasks (it said "None taken on"). The town margin no longer lists a gunsmith or jeweller: no card leads to either (the bowyer sells the guns; Jewelers' Avenue was cut from the game, its card unfinished). Under the hood: docs/PORT_STRUCTURE.md.

**New in 1.1.1:** fixes a crash on loading a game with the side panels on (wide screens). The game's own questions ("Quit to DOS? y/n") and the Load screen opened from a card now show over the new card screen. The battle map scrolls at a steady pace (Shift at the edge no longer flies to the end of the field), and resting the pointer on the edge scrolls without Shift. After a battle, javelins, throwing axes, knives and darts go back to whoever threw them. The battle log tells missed shots and throws, and shots the armour stopped.

**New in 1.1:** the cards are drawn by Darklands+ itself (F12 > Helpers, on by default; "Play the original game" turns it off). The game still decides everything on a card, but its text is now set in the chosen typeface at any window size instead of being redrawn over the game's letters. The options are rows to click, with their number key, their chance of success beside them, and greyed when they cannot be chosen. A large illuminated initial opens each text. An option that uses an item or calls on a saint lists, under it, who in the party can, and a tooltip says which saints or items the card takes even when nobody has them. No more clicking once more before the options appear. Under the hood: docs/NATIVE_SCREENS.md, the start of replacing the game's interface piece by piece.

**New in 1.0.1:** travel on the overland map no longer stops for a moment every second (the emulated DOS clock went back briefly once a second, and the game's pause between steps waited for it to catch up; battles and other timed pauses were affected the same way).

### What it adds

- **Text and display:** crisp text at any window size up to 4K, in a choice of seven typefaces (a readable gothic one, Grenze Gotisch, by default). The pointer follows the mouse smoothly. Wide-screen margins show the date, place, purse and tasks on one side, and on the other the town you are in or near (reputation, buildings, shops and their quality), the blessings and potions at work and who carries too much. One setting sizes the margins, tooltips and map names. The window opens maximized (it remembers if you make it smaller). Full screen with Alt+Enter. PNG screenshots with Ctrl+F12 (it also writes the window sizes into the log, for reports). The MicroProse logo and opening animations play the first time only (F12 > Display plays them at every start). The title menu shows the Darklands+ version.
- **No more changing the leader:** a card's test of the leader alone (every talk with its charisma and Speak Common, a lore, a perception ...) is made by the member who does it best, whose name and chance are shown beside the option, and shops price by the party's best charisma ("Ursula bargains"); the leader you chose is back after the card. F12 > Helpers.
- **One pack for the whole party:** each member's sheet shows what they use, then the party's pack: every spare thing of every member, by kind and quality. Ready or Drink takes from the pack (Darklands+ hands the thing over first), shops list and sell the pack (Sell spare gear sells the whole party's), and before a battle each archer, crossbowman and gunner is handed their ammunition and the potions are shared out among the members (in a battle one drinks only what one carries). **Keep** marks a thing never to be sold or dropped. Pointing at the load under the doll explains what it does (only what is in use weighs: Agility in card tests and in fending off blows, the pace of swings). F12 > Helpers.
- **Money with coins:** every amount Darklands+ writes (the purse, shops, inns, the loot, the journal, Party Info) shows its florins, groschen and pfennigs each with a small gold, silver or copper coin, and a price described below a shop's lists (or the purse under the pointer) also gives the whole amount in pfennigs, so two prices compare at a glance (1 florin = 20 groschen = 240 pfennigs). F12 > Display switches back to 1fl 12gr 4pf.
- **Tooltips everywhere:** point at an item, a saint or an alchemy formula on any of the game's screens (shops, loot, character sheet, equipment, saints lists, the formula book, a card's list of saints and items) to see what it does. Saint descriptions show the effect in its own colour.
- **Cards:** drawn by Darklands+ (the text in the chosen typeface, the options as rows to click, an illuminated initial; switchable). Chances of success next to the options (only where the party's skills decide). Number keys pick options. The saints and items a card accepts are shown. "Go to" in cities (G) walks the party to a shop, church or gate.
- **Journal:** a chronicle of every text the cards have shown (word for word, with the date, place and your choice, searchable), your own notes for each game, a quest log, a town journal, a saints finder, alchemy formulas, where to find goods and ingredients, and what the party has left in store rooms anywhere.
- **Travel:** a zoomable world map (M) and auto-travel: Ctrl+click a place and the party goes there. Towns and places are named on the travel map. Random encounters on the road can be made rarer (or more frequent). So can the cutthroats and the watch in a town's streets at night (Settings, Travel): with little Stealth and Streetwise, leaving the night side streets meets thieves about 8 times in 10.
- **Battles:** a battle log, info under the pointer (a foe's weapons, its armour and whether each of your weapons pierces it, is halved or deflected), a click on an enemy sends the selected member to attack it (Std Attack) without first choosing an attack from the order menu, a thin bar over each fighter with the endurance left (and the strength once it is down by half), a pause when someone falls, adjustable battle speed, better pathfinding, and a map of the level (Tab), where a click on a floor, a door or stairs the party has seen walks the party there (in single file, the leader first, so that a passage or a wall does not jam the formation; G travels as a group again).
- **Party and inventory:** the whole party side by side with drag and drop, item tooltips with weapon numbers and who can use them, a one-click leader change, and "Tidy the pack" (sorts a member's or everyone's items by kind and name). On the loot screen, A takes the whole pile and S shares it out among the party, instead of one G per arrow; in shops, A sells a whole stack and T buys ten, and **Sell spare gear** sells, after a second click, every weapon, armour and shield the member carries but does not use (not ammunition or things to throw), with the total shown first.
- **More figures for the party:** Select Character Image offers the enemies' human figures too: the grey-haired, brown-haired and blond ex-adventurers, a sergeant, town guard, mercenary, bandit, knight, knight in plate, Templar, monk, alchemist, witch and cultist (18 figures instead of 4), all shown at once to pick from. Each walks, fights and dies with its own animations and has colours of its own for the 1st/2nd/3rd color keys (red, blue, green, gold ...; hair keeps its colour). A figure without an animation for the weapon in hand swings the nearest one it has.
- **Saves:** quick save (Ctrl+S) and quick load (F9), autosaves on arrival and before battles, and a save browser.
- **Fixes for bugs of 483.07:**
  - blessings that cost attribute points for good
  - items lost to a full pack
  - "(null)" items
  - the "neon hair" colours
  - Save and Load greyed out after the inn
  - weapon potions that never wear off
  - quests off the edge of the map
  - robbers corrupting the item count (a crash later)
  - items lost in overfull store rooms
  - and more, each with its own switch (F12 > Helpers)
- **Restored:** the Honor Roll of Glory, drawn by MicroProse but never reachable.
- **Built-in mods** (off by default): swift travel, half-price inns, quick learning, heavy blows, a strong Quickstart party, a rich start.
- **Roland MT-32 music:** Darklands was composed for the MT-32 / LAPC-I, and Munt's emulator is now built in. Give it the synthesizer's two ROM files (if you use Munt or DOSBox with an MT-32 you have them; Darklands+ finds them in Munt's and DOSBox Staging's ROM folders by itself) and the music and battle sounds play as composed, with no MIDI setup. Without the ROMs the emulated Sound Blaster plays, and Darklands+ explains at start why the MT-32 is worth it and lets you pick the ROMs' folder (with "Don't remind me again"). With both a CM-32L and an MT-32 pair the MT-32 plays by default (on the CM-32L the battle sounds come out as chimes instead of blows and grunts); you can switch between them while playing. What the game writes on the synthesizer's display ("[ MPS Darklands ]") is shown for a moment, as on the real one.
- **Sortable tables:** a click on a column's heading sorts the item list, the enemies, the weapons, the party's items, the alchemy formulas and the shops' wares (Shift+click for a second column).
- **Enemies (F12 > Records):** every foe with its attributes, weapon skills, armour and how hard it is (which weapons pierce it), shield, missile and melee weapons; editable and saved as a mod.
- **Game controllers:** an Xbox, PlayStation or Switch pad, or a Steam Deck, plays the game: the left stick moves the pointer, A clicks, B opens the menu bar, X / Y are Enter / Esc, the d-pad the arrow keys, Start the Darklands+ menu, Back the world map (F1 lists them all). Off in F12 > Helpers.
- **Real MT-32s and other MIDI synthesizers:** the "Roland MIDI" setting can send to any MIDI output Windows has (a real MT-32 on a MIDI interface, Munt's driver, a Sound Canvas), chosen in F12 > Display.
- **Music volume:** its own slider (F12 > Display), for the FM and the MT-32 alike.
- **Music packs:** a mod (or a "music" folder) can replace the game's pieces of music with recordings (Ogg or WAV), piece by piece; F12 > Display > Sound names each piece as it starts. None comes with Darklands+.
- **Battle sounds:** a mod with real recordings (swords clashing, shields, blows, cries, the bow, the handgun, a war horn for an ambush) in place of the synthesizer notes comes with the download; tick it in F12 > Mods. The recordings are from 0 A.D. (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- **New encounters:** sixteen new wilderness encounters in the spirit of the original come with the download as a mod (tick it in F12 > Mods): a mandrake under the gallows oak, a herb-wife, flagellants, a robber knight's bridge toll, the lord's deer and his huntsmen, kobolds at a mine, will-o'-the-wisps in the marsh, a procession of the dead, an overturned wagon that may be bait, a lost child, a relic seller, charcoal burners, grave robbers, a peasants' boar hunt, a camp of deserters and a knight's feud. They are cards like the game's own, with its pictures, saints, potions, skill checks and battles, balanced against the original encounters, and they come up among them about as often as one of the game's own. A small red seal on the card marks a new one. Mods can add their own: an encounter is a text file with a short script (docs/MODDING.md).
- **Modding:** mod folders for game data, single card texts, menu texts, weapon numbers, items, pictures and sounds; mods switch on and off at once, sounds included. F12 > Mods makes a new mod folder with instructions.

### If something goes wrong

Darklands+ writes `darklands_port.log` in the game folder; it says why a start failed. There is no official support: the project has been handed over to the community (fork it and carry on). Some antivirus programs distrust files a compiler has just made. If yours blocks `darklands_game.dll`, allow it; it is made from your own `DARKLAND.EXE` by the TinyCC in `darklands_plus\tcc`. On Linux and Steam Deck the Windows zip runs under Wine or Proton.
