Darklands+ runs Darklands (MicroProse, 1992) natively on Windows, without DOSBox, from your own copy of the game. It adds the things players have asked for over the years, and every addition can be switched off. **F12 > Settings > Helpers** offers presets, from **Buggy Purist** (the game exactly as it was in 1993) and **Purist** (the same with its bugs fixed) through the standard **Darklands+** to **Easy**, and every switch one by one.

**You need** your own Darklands 483.07 (GOG or Steam) and 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. This download contains nothing from the game.

**Install:** unpack the zip anywhere and start `darklands.exe`. It finds a GOG or Steam installation by itself or asks for the folder once. The first start takes about two minutes while Darklands+ translates the game program from your `DARKLAND.EXE`; after that it starts at once. Your saved games stay where they always were. `Darklands+ README.txt` in the zip has the details.

**New in 1.2**

- **A new start screen** in the game's own art: **Continue** loads your newest save in one click.
- **The intro** ends at once on a key or a click; later starts show only the lady's welcome (or the whole intro, or nothing).
- **Most of the game's screens are redrawn** for the mouse and today's screens (each can be switched off): the shops, the character sheet, the loot, the store room, the inn and camping, alchemy, calling on a saint, Party Info, the marching order, Save and Load, Enemy Info and the menus.
- **Making a character** fills the screen: all 37 professions at once by kind, with what each needs, what your character lacks for it, what it leads to and what it teaches; the skill step shows each skill's room.
- **Choosing the party:** each member's figure and numbers, and the party at its best in each skill, the gaps in red.
- **Saves:** a new save can take the place of an old one (a click on it in the list), and the Load page deletes a save.
- **The best member speaks for the party:** card tests of "the leader" are made by whoever does them best.
- **One pack for the whole party**, and **one store room for every inn**.
- **Quests at a glance:** a short title, the days left and a picture of its kind for each, in the margin, the journal and on the maps. A witches' Sabbat the party hears of becomes a task, its place marked. In a town, Go To (G) marks the house a task leads to.
- **Where to learn a saint and buy a formula:** each library and alchemist has fixed lists for the whole game, and Darklands+ names the nearest one for each the party lacks. Under a town's name: what it has that most towns lack, and its notable shops.
- **Cards** say why an option is greyed (about 280 reasons) and mark the options that start a fight.
- **Battles:** **Z** fast-forwards; orders as buttons in the margin, and each member's order in words on the party panel; a bar over every fighter; and "Victory! The battle is won." at the end instead of eight seconds of a still field.
- **Castles, dungeons and mines** show the foes still waiting there, floor by floor, and mark the master of the place, whose fall wins it, or name the floor where he waits; the battle pauses when a group of them comes out to fight.
- **Shops and loot** set a weapon or an armour against what the party has; an ingredient shows the potions it goes into.
- **Arrows, quarrels and balls never run out**, and thrown weapons come back after a battle.
- **Potions with pictures:** colour, emblem and the grade as dots.
- **F12 > Guide** explains the game for new players.
- **Sound:** fixed a loud screech and the music cutting out after loading a game when the intro had played to its end.
- **Fixed (a bug of Darklands+ 1.0 to 1.1.1):** with the Blessings fix on, a saint's lowering of an attribute (some saints lower Perception, Intellect, Agility or Charisma for a day as the price of their help) never wore off. It now does; points already lost this way can be put back in F12 > Editor.
- **Fixes for bugs of 483.07:** inn teachers who charged "-4096pfs", worn armour that took identical spares down with it, card options greyed by mistake, debt notes never where the merchant said they were, a formula's risk the wrong way round, and more.

Every change in detail: `docs/changes-1.2.md`.

**New in 1.1.1:** fixes a crash on loading a game with the side panels on (wide screens). The game's own questions ("Quit to DOS? y/n") and the Load screen opened from a card now show over the new card screen. The battle map scrolls at a steady pace (Shift at the edge no longer flies to the end of the field), and resting the pointer on the edge scrolls without Shift. After a battle, javelins, throwing axes, knives and darts go back to whoever threw them. The battle log tells missed shots and throws, and shots the armour stopped.

**New in 1.1:** the cards are drawn by Darklands+ itself (F12 > Settings > Helpers, on by default; "Play the original game" turns it off). The game still decides everything on a card, but its text is now set in the chosen typeface at any window size instead of being redrawn over the game's letters. The options are rows to click, with their number key, their chance of success beside them, and greyed when they cannot be chosen. A large illuminated initial opens each text. An option that uses an item or calls on a saint lists, under it, who in the party can, and a tooltip says which saints or items the card takes even when nobody has them. No more clicking once more before the options appear. Under the hood: docs/NATIVE_SCREENS.md, the start of replacing the game's interface piece by piece.

**New in 1.0.1:** travel on the overland map no longer stops for a moment every second (the emulated DOS clock went back briefly once a second, and the game's pause between steps waited for it to catch up; battles and other timed pauses were affected the same way).

### What it adds

- **Text and display:** crisp text at any window size up to 4K, in a choice of seven typefaces (a readable gothic one, Grenze Gotisch, by default). The pointer follows the mouse smoothly. Wide-screen margins show the date, place, purse and tasks on one side, and on the other the town you are in or near (reputation, what it has that most towns lack, its notable shops), and the blessings and potions at work; a member who carries too much has a weight on their card. One setting sizes the margins, tooltips and map names. The window opens maximized (it remembers if you make it smaller). Full screen with Alt+Enter. PNG screenshots with Ctrl+F12 (it also writes the window sizes into the log, for reports). The whole opening (the MicroProse logo and the animations) plays the first time; after that the game starts with its last scene, the lady of the Darklands and her welcome (F12 > Settings > Display: the whole opening, the last scene or nothing). A key or a click ends the opening at once (it used to wait for the end of the animation playing). The title menu shows the Darklands+ version.
- **No more changing the leader:** a card's test of the leader alone (every talk with its charisma and Speak Common, a lore, a perception ...) is made by the member who does it best, whose name and chance are shown beside the option, and shops price by the party's best charisma ("Ursula bargains"); the leader you chose is back after the card. F12 > Settings > Helpers.
- **One pack for the whole party:** each member keeps only what they use (and the horse or mule they ride) and their own potions; everything else is the party's pack, every spare thing of every member by kind and quality. The sheet shows In use, then Potions, then the pack. Ready takes from the pack (Darklands+ hands the thing over first), shops list and sell the pack (Sell spare gear sells the whole party's), the loot and an inn's store room show the member's potions and the pack beside the pile or the store (a potion goes to the member shown, the rest into the pack; Put away stores the pack but what is kept and the potions). The game itself still keeps each thing with some member, so where it asks one member for something Darklands+ hands it over first: lock picks to whoever opens a lock, a spare horse or mule to whoever has none (so the party counts as mounted). A member who leaves, or dies on a card, leaves their spares in the pack. With ammunition that never runs out, arrows, quarrels and balls are not shown at all (each shooter quietly carries the one the game needs). **Keep** marks a thing never to be sold or dropped. Pointing at the load under the doll explains what it does (only what is in use weighs: Agility in card tests and in fending off blows, the pace of swings). F12 > Settings > Helpers.
- **Money with coins:** every amount Darklands+ writes (the purse, shops, inns, the loot, the journal, Party Info) shows its florins, groschen and pfennigs each with a small gold, silver or copper coin, and a price described below a shop's lists (or the purse under the pointer) also gives the whole amount in pfennigs, so two prices compare at a glance (1 florin = 20 groschen = 240 pfennigs). F12 > Settings > Display switches back to 1fl 12gr 4pf.
- **Tooltips everywhere:** point at an item, a saint or an alchemy formula on any of the game's screens (shops, loot, character sheet, equipment, saints lists, the formula book, a card's list of saints and items) to see what it does. Saint descriptions show the effect in its own colour.
- **Cards:** drawn by Darklands+ (the text in the chosen typeface, the options as rows to click, an illuminated initial; switchable). Chances of success next to the options (only where the party's skills decide). Number keys pick options. The saints and items a card accepts are shown. "Go to" in cities (G) walks the party to a shop, church or gate.
- **Journal:** a chronicle of every text the cards have shown (word for word, with the date, place and your choice, searchable), your own notes for each game, a quest log (which also notes a witches' Sabbat once a card has told the party its night or its place, the place marked on the map), a town journal, a saints finder, alchemy formulae, where to find goods and ingredients, and what the party has left in store rooms anywhere.
- **Travel:** a zoomable world map (M) and auto-travel: Ctrl+click a place and the party goes there. Towns and places are named on the travel map. Random encounters on the road can be made rarer (or more frequent). So can the cutthroats and the watch in a town's streets at night (Settings, Travel): with little Stealth and Streetwise, leaving the night side streets meets thieves about 8 times in 10.
- **Battles:** a battle log, info under the pointer (a foe's weapons, its armour and whether each of your weapons pierces it, is halved or deflected), a click on an enemy sends the selected member to attack it (Std Attack) without first choosing an attack from the order menu, a thin bar over each fighter with the endurance left (and the strength once it is down by half), a pause when someone falls, adjustable battle speed, better pathfinding, and a map of the level (Tab), where a click on a floor, a door or stairs the party has seen walks the party there (in single file, the leader first, so that a passage or a wall does not jam the formation; G travels as a group again). A shooter whose line of fire is blocked (by a friend, a foe or a corner) steps aside to the nearest spot a few paces away with a clear line and shoots from there, instead of waiting; any foe can be chosen as the target even with no clear line (the game refused it with a barred circle and a beep): with no spot near, the shooter walks towards it until a line opens (experimental, off by default). If you like, the party's archers, crossbowmen and gunners shoot past their own fighters instead (in Darklands a missile only ever strikes its target; off by default). **Z** fast-forwards a battle (six times the pace, for the easy fights) and Z again returns to the chosen speed. In a castle, dungeon or mine the margin also says how many foes still wait there, not yet met, on this floor and on the others, and marks the master of the place, or says on which floor he waits (once he has fallen and his fight is over, the place is won: the game ends a castle's or dungeon's battle only so, or when the party walks out and flees). At the end, the box the game shows ("BattleResults - victory", the field held still for eight seconds) says "Victory! The battle is won." and stays three seconds.
- **Party and inventory:** the whole party side by side with drag and drop, item tooltips with weapon numbers and who can use them, a one-click leader change, and each pack shown in order by kind and name. On the loot screen, A takes the whole pile and S shares it out among the party, instead of one G per arrow; in shops, A sells a whole stack and T buys ten, and **Sell spare gear** sells, after a second click, every weapon, armour and shield the member carries but does not use (not ammunition or things to throw), with the total shown first.
- **More figures for the party:** Select Character Image offers the enemies' human figures too: the grey-haired, brown-haired and blond ex-adventurers, a sergeant, town guard, mercenary, bandit, knight, knight in plate, Templar, monk, alchemist, witch and cultist (18 figures instead of 4), all shown at once to pick from. Each walks, fights and dies with its own animations and has colours of its own for the 1st/2nd/3rd color keys (red, blue, green, gold ...; hair keeps its colour). A figure without an animation for the weapon in hand swings the nearest one it has.
- **Saves:** quick save (Ctrl+S) and quick load (F9), autosaves on arrival and before battles, and a save browser.
- **Fixes for bugs of 483.07:**
  - blessings that cost attribute points for good
  - items lost to a full pack
  - "(null)" items
  - the "neon hair" colours
  - Save and Load greyed out after the inn
  - weapon potions that never wear off
  - quests off the edge of the map
  - robbers corrupting the item count (a crash later)
  - items lost in overfull store rooms
  - and more, each with its own switch (F12 > Settings > Helpers)
- **Restored:** the Honor Roll of Glory, drawn by MicroProse but never reachable.
- **Built-in mods** (off by default): swift travel, half-price inns, quick learning, heavy blows, a strong Quickstart party, a rich start.
- **Roland MT-32 music:** Darklands was composed for the MT-32 / LAPC-I, and Munt's emulator is now built in. Give it the synthesizer's two ROM files (if you use Munt or DOSBox with an MT-32 you have them; Darklands+ finds them in Munt's and DOSBox Staging's ROM folders by itself) and the music and battle sounds play as composed, with no MIDI setup. Without the ROMs the emulated Sound Blaster plays, and Darklands+ explains at start why the MT-32 is worth it and lets you pick the ROMs' folder (with "Don't remind me again"). With both a CM-32L and an MT-32 pair the MT-32 plays by default (on the CM-32L the battle sounds come out as chimes instead of blows and grunts); you can switch between them while playing. What the game writes on the synthesizer's display ("[ MPS Darklands ]") is shown for a moment, as on the real one.
- **Sortable tables:** a click on a column's heading sorts the item list, the enemies, the weapons, the party's items, the alchemy formulae and the shops' wares (Shift+click for a second column).
- **Enemies (F12 > Records):** every foe with its attributes, weapon skills, armour and how hard it is (which weapons pierce it), shield, missile and melee weapons; editable and saved as a mod.
- **Game controllers:** an Xbox, PlayStation or Switch pad, or a Steam Deck, plays the game: the left stick moves the pointer, A clicks, B opens the menu bar, X / Y are Enter / Esc, the d-pad the arrow keys, Start the Darklands+ menu, Back the world map (F1 lists them all). Off in F12 > Settings > Helpers.
- **Real MT-32s and other MIDI synthesizers:** the "Roland MIDI" setting can send to any MIDI output Windows has (a real MT-32 on a MIDI interface, Munt's driver, a Sound Canvas), chosen in F12 > Settings > Display.
- **Music volume:** its own slider (F12 > Settings > Display), for the FM and the MT-32 alike.
- **Music packs:** a mod (or a "music" folder) can replace the game's pieces of music with recordings (Ogg or WAV), piece by piece; F12 > Settings > Display > Sound names each piece as it starts. None comes with Darklands+.
- **Battle sounds:** a mod with real recordings (swords clashing, shields, blows, cries, the bow, the handgun, a war horn for an ambush) in place of the synthesizer notes comes with the download; tick it in F12 > Settings > Mods. The recordings are from 0 A.D. (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- **New encounters:** sixteen new wilderness encounters in the spirit of the original come with the download as a mod (tick it in F12 > Settings > Mods): a mandrake under the gallows oak, a herb-wife, flagellants, a robber knight's bridge toll, the lord's deer and his huntsmen, kobolds at a mine, will-o'-the-wisps in the marsh, a procession of the dead, an overturned wagon that may be bait, a lost child, a relic seller, charcoal burners, grave robbers, a peasants' boar hunt, a camp of deserters and a knight's feud. They are cards like the game's own, with its pictures, saints, potions, skill checks and battles, balanced against the original encounters, and they come up among them about as often as one of the game's own. A small red seal on the card marks a new one. Mods can add their own: an encounter is a text file with a short script (docs/MODDING.md).
- **Modding:** mod folders for game data, single card texts, menu texts, weapon numbers, items, pictures and sounds; mods switch on and off at once, sounds included. F12 > Settings > Mods makes a new mod folder with instructions.

### Limits kept from the original

These are part of the game's memory and of every saved game, so Darklands+ works within them rather than changing them (a change would break existing saves):

- 64 kinds of thing per member, each kind a stack of up to 255 (with one pack for the party, that is 64 kinds for each member, and new things go to whoever has room);
- 100 kinds of thing in a town's store room (more is refused, with a word why);
- 20 kinds of throwing potion in a member's battle orders;
- five members in the party.

### If something goes wrong

Darklands+ writes `darklands_port.log` in the game folder; it says why a start failed. There is no official support: the project has been handed over to the community (fork it and carry on). Some antivirus programs distrust files a compiler has just made. If yours blocks `darklands_game.dll`, allow it; it is made from your own `DARKLAND.EXE` by the TinyCC in `darklands_plus\tcc`. On Linux and Steam Deck the Windows zip runs under Wine or Proton.
